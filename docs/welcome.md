# Welcome to Passalong

Hey, glad you're here. Passalong is our little neighborhood shelf for Yoto cards. The idea is simple: a card your kid has outgrown might be brand new to another family, so instead of everyone buying doubles, we pass them around.

It's invite-only and private. Nothing you have is shared with anyone until you choose to share it.

## Getting in

1. Tap the invite link a neighbor sent you and enter the code.
2. Connect your Yoto account. You log in on Yoto's own page, so we never see your password, and you can disconnect anytime.
3. Your commercial cards sync in on their own. Pick a username (no real name needed) and agree to our community promises.

That's it. You're on the shelf.

## Put it on your phone

Do this so it can nudge you when a card's due back or someone wants to borrow.

- On iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen."
- Open it from that new icon and tap "Enable notifications."

## What stays private

- We only ever see store-bought cards. Your Make Your Own recordings, the ones with grandma reading a bedtime story, are never visible to this app. That's built in, not a setting you have to find.
- Everything you sync starts private. You choose what to offer, one card at a time or all at once.
- No addresses, no phone numbers, nothing about your kids. Neighbors see a rough sense of how close you are, never a pin on a map.

## How it works

- **Offer what you're happy to share.** On your shelf, tap "Offer" on any card. Keep the precious ones home, or mark them off-limits so they show without anyone asking.
- **Say what you want.** Add cards to your wishlist. When a neighbor is offering one, it shows up in your Matches.
- **Ask for it.** Send a borrow or swap request. The owner gets a nudge and can say yes or no.
- **Sort out the handoff in the app.** Message each other to pick a time and place. No need to trade numbers.
- **Return it as you got it.** Borrows have a return-by date, and you'll get a friendly reminder before it's due.

## A few promises we make each other

Move in good faith. Look after each other's families. Only lend what you'd be at peace losing, because kids are kids and things happen. Assume the best of your neighbors. If something feels off, tell the organizer.

## Something not right?

Tap "Report" anytime and the organizer will see it. We'd rather hear about it early.

Thanks for passing things along.
